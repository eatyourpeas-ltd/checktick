# 360° colleague feedback battery {360-colleague-feedback}

Give feedback on the clinician you are rating. Tick one box on each line. Where a line does not apply to your experience of them, tick "Does not apply".

## In what capacity do you know this clinician? {rater-capacity}
(mc_single)
- Consultant / attending colleague
- Direct managerial supervisor
- Junior colleague or trainee
- Other healthcare professional
- Administrative or clerical colleague
- Patient or carer

## How long have you worked with this clinician? {rater-duration}
(mc_single)
- Less than 3 months
- 3–12 months
- 1–3 years
- More than 3 years
